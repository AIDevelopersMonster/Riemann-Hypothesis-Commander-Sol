## Description

We introduce a phase graph on noncollinear triples of a Steiner triple system, distinguishing projective `S_7`, Hall `S_9`, and defective local closure types. Spectral expansion of the independent-triple graph, combined with anti-mitre witness charging and a localized finite phase-interface argument, yields a quantitative Hall/projective phase-profile stability theorem: the minority pure phase is bounded linearly by the normalized anti-mitre count,

`min(rho_P,rho_H) <= C c_A/N`,

for an absolute constant `C`. The zero-defect case recovers the exact projective/Hall dichotomy. The result concerns phase-profile stability and does not assert edit-distance proximity of the full block set to a projective or Hall Steiner triple system.

The publication also corrects an identification error in the preceding research seed: the mirror-completion residual is five-line configuration #7, whereas the Král anti-mitre `C_A` is configuration #4; their occurrence counts satisfy exactly `c_A=2r_7`.
