# SOL-SELECTOR Research State

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: OPEN / foundational stage

## Current strongest structural claim

The minimal preservation-only morphism layer does not force a `BR/B0` bifurcation. Instead the first candidate structure is the directed quotient pattern

```math
F_{\mathrm{mix}}\longrightarrow B0\longrightarrow BR.
```

The branch will test whether this survives a fully formal treatment of partiality, reflection, translation, and protected undefinedness.

## What is frozen

- old core `M0` is fixed pointwise;
- root is fixed;
- old defined values are preserved;
- translation/reflection equivariance is preserved;
- protected old undefined cells remain protected;
- mixed-frontier outputs may be identified by morphisms;
- no output-sort distinction is built into the first morphism layer.

## What is not yet frozen

- whether admissible objects must define every mixed cell or may define only part of the mixed frontier;
- exact categorical encoding of protected non-definedness (object axiom vs relational expansion vs forbidden-domain predicate);
- whether translations act on new outputs, and if so whether action is total or partial;
- whether `relation-only` means invariance under the full automorphism group of the mixed relation or literal dependence on one relation symbol;
- congruence notion for partial algebras with protected holes;
- behavior once re-entry/closure is introduced.

## Publication status

Not publication-ready. The line has a potentially novel theorem package, but T1/T2 and the quotient comparison require formal proof and hostile audit before any publication threshold is declared.
