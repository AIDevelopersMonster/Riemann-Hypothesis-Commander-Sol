## Frozen theorem nucleus

The release version freezes the one-step theorem package at

```math
M_0 \to F_{\to} \to F_{\mathrm{mix}} \to B0 \to BR,
```

with

```math
\theta_{span}\parallel\theta_{gap},
```

```math
\theta_{span}\wedge\theta_{gap}=\theta_{orb},
```

```math
\theta_{span}\vee\theta_{gap}=\theta_{phase}.
```

It also includes:

- uniqueness of `BR` as the reflection-compatible base-line anchor of `B0`;
- the incompatible-anchor no-common-upper-bound theorem;
- the exact weak/strong bifurcation boundary for `B0 -> BR`.

## Claim discipline

The release explicitly does not claim novelty for preservation-only partial-algebra homomorphisms, weak quotient constructions, free completions, or congruence lattices of `C2`/G-sets.

The contribution is scoped to the combined FCOA selector architecture, its canonically generated span/gap quotient geometry, and the transition from pure event quotient lattice behavior to constrained anchored-poset behavior.

No claim is made that span/gap/phase are uniformly parameter-free FO-definable in the weakest infinite reduct.
