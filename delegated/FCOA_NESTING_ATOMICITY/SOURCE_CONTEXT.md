## Conceptual starting point

The main FCOA programme treats a fixed carrier, partial operations, terminal outputs, orientation, and erasure tests as distinct structural layers. This branch studies the earlier conceptual claim that atomicity should be defined only relative to a sandbox of allowed compositions.

The historical classical example is multiplicative atomicity of positive integers, but that example is illustrative only. Do not import unique factorization, divisibility, or classical prime behavior into FCOA without explicit definitions.
