## L4B — Non-Cartesian candidate

Study:

\[

This branch may connect more naturally to groupoids, quivers, braid-like systems, connections, or holonomy than to Cartesian geometry.

### Important correction to reviewer forecast

Two reversible directions do not by themselves imply \(\mathbb Z^2\). Commutation/independence/normal-form hypotheses are required.

# Literature-context rung — group completion

The reviewer correctly suggests comparison with group completion, but the relation must be stated carefully.

Classically,

\[
(\mathbb N_0,+)\to(\mathbb Z,+)
\]

is the Grothendieck/group completion of a commutative monoid.

FCOA-Z does **not** start with a monoid operation \(+\) and group-complete it. It starts with a pointed set carrying a one-sided shift and partial legacy operations, then minimally makes the shift reversible.

Thus the safe statement is:

\[
\boxed{\text{the carrier completion is analogous to, but not identified with, Grothendieck group completion}.}
\]

A precise universal-property comparison is a future theorem target.
