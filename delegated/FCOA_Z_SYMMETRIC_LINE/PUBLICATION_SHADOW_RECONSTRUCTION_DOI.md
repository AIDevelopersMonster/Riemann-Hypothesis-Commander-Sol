1. Direct associative realization preserving the audited radial `oplus` cells collapses the base coordinates.
2. Existing one-dimensional `otimes` translations isolate the root.
3. Shift conjugation of the root projector yields matrix units `E_ij` and the finitary matrix algebra `M_fin(Z,K)`, containing `M_n(K)` for every finite `n`.
4. The finitary matrix algebra alone forgets line geometry.
5. `(I,U)` reconstructs the oriented two-sided line up to translation.
6. `(I,U,V)` reconstructs the rooted signed line.
7. The generated base algebra does not reconstruct primitive-operation provenance.
8. Base-only shadows do not reconstruct the distinction between true `UNDEF` cells and erased terminal-valued cells.
9. Canonical `share` and `split` terminal lifts remain indistinguishable at the base-shadow level and require terminal involution/incidence data to separate.
