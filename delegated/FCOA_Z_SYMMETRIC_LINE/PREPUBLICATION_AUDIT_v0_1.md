## 1. Theorem completeness

PASS.

Every result promoted in the draft has a proof in the article itself:

- Minimal Reversible Completion;
- Derived Reflection;
- Symmetry-Induced Radialization;
- preservation of role asymmetry/noncommutativity;
- Mixed-Sector Localization.

No mixed-sector interaction law is promoted as a theorem.

## 2. Arithmetic firewall

PASS.

The paper does not use ordinary binary `+` or `times` as primitive FCOA operations. The external identification with the integer line is explicitly structural.

## 3. Main novelty boundary

PASS WITH CAUTION.

The paper explicitly excludes novelty claims for:

- natural/minimal invertible extension of a one-sided shift;
- the integer line itself;
- reflection symmetry as a classical object;
- graded or sector-dependent algebra in general;
- Jordan/associative pairs;
- partial associative composition;
- QFT locality.

Candidate contribution is narrower: transport of a pre-existing role-sensitive partial FCOA operation through the reversible completion, producing the radialization theorem and mixed-sector localization in the same carrier.

Priority is not yet established.

## 4. Strongest theorem statement safe for publication

The safest central statement is:

> A one-sided FCOA predecessor-type right-zero rule, when transported to the minimal reversible completion under root reflection equivariance, becomes a radial contraction: the same right translation is predecessor on the positive half-line and successor on the negative half-line. Once the positive legacy sector is fixed, all additional binary base-operation freedom is confined to mixed-sign sectors.

This is proved and does not depend on any speculative mixed-sector rule.

## 5. Claims that must remain out of theorem language

- “commutativity and associativity are not fundamental laws”;
- “FCOA-Z models physical space”;
- “the number line creates quantum statistics”;
- “mixed-sign interaction necessarily becomes associative or commutative”;
- “the construction is the first sector-dependent algebra.”

These may appear only as motivation/research questions with explicit qualification.

## 7. Structural weaknesses remaining

1. The reversible-completion theorem is intentionally simple/classical in flavor; the article must keep the focus on the operation-transfer theorem.
2. `reflection equivariance` is still a chosen extension principle. It is not yet derived from the operation itself. This is acceptable for the first paper but must be stated as an assumption.
3. The article does not yet prove a mixed-sign commutativity/associativity phase split. It proves only directional-law differentiation and localization of future freedom.
4. The exact relation between the older mirror-copy formulation and the new reversible-completion formulation should be presented as equivalence/canonical identification, not as two independent constructions.
