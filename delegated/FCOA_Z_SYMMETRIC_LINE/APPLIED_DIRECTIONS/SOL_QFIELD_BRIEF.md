# SOL-QFIELD — Scientific Director Brief

## Scope
Pauli exclusion, fermionic exchange symmetry, particle–antiparticle channels, annihilation in QFT.

## Core question
Can the FCOA distinction between carrier-preserving interactions and terminal/transverse output channels illuminate a rigorous abstract distinction between exchange constraints and channel conversion?

## First target
Separate three notions that must not be conflated:

1. antisymmetry of many-fermion states under exchange;
2. (anti)commutation relations of field operators;
3. particle–antiparticle annihilation as an allowed scattering/reaction channel.

Then test whether FCOA has structurally distinct objects corresponding to these roles rather than forcing them into one binary law.

## Required negative test
Show whether the current FCOA operations lack amplitudes, Hilbert/Fock space, conservation laws, and probabilistic channel weights needed for genuine QFT modeling.

## Success criterion
A precise abstract channel taxonomy useful for FCOA, or a rejection of the annihilation analogy at the present level.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not equate Pauli exclusion with noncommutativity and do not infer commutativity from annihilation into bosonic final states.
