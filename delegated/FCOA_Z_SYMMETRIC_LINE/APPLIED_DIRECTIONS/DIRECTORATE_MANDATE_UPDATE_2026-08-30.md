## E-output rule

No \(E\)-family is to be called a second coordinate, spatial dimension, imaginary axis, quantum state, photon, fusion channel, or internal degree of freedom merely because it is transverse to the base sort.

Each family must first be classified as one of:

- terminal label only;
- finite/internal fiber over a line point;
- re-enterable state;
- transport state;
- independently iterable coordinate candidate;
- inconsistent with conservative realization.

Only an independently iterable, unbounded, conservative direction can qualify as a serious emergent-dimension candidate.
