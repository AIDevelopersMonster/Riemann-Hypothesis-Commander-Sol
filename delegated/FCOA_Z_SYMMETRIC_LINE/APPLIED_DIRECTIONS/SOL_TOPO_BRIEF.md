# SOL-TOPO — Scientific Director Brief

## Scope
Non-Abelian anyons, braid groups, fusion categories, topological memory.

## Core question
Can FCOA mixed-sector interactions and typed terminal outputs be mapped to fusion-channel structure while preserving order/path memory?

## First target
Build a toy comparison with a simple fusion category (e.g. Ising-type rules) and test whether FCOA output fibers can encode multiple outcomes rather than a single scalar result.

## Required negative test
Determine whether braid/path information is fundamentally absent from one-line FCOA unless additional transport/history structure is added.

## Success criterion
Either a faithful finite toy embedding of fusion-channel behavior or a theorem that current one-line FCOA cannot represent braiding memory.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not assert that anyon/anti-anyon interaction is automatically trivial, uniquely vacuum-valued, or commutative.
