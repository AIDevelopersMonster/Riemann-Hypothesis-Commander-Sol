# SOL-LOCALITY — Scientific Director Brief

## Scope
Algebraic quantum field theory, local nets, microcausality, geometry-conditioned commutation.

## Core question
Can FCOA sector-local law differentiation be formulated as a discrete one-dimensional analogue of locality: algebraic compatibility determined by geometric relation of supports/participants rather than by a global commutativity axiom?

## First target
Define a discrete locality predicate on the FCOA axis and compare

\[
L(x,y)\Longrightarrow C_\star(x,y)=EQ
\]

with Haag–Kastler locality for spacelike-separated local observable algebras.

## Required negative test
Show exactly which ingredients of AQFT (causal structure, local nets, isotony, covariance, state space) are absent from present FCOA-Z and therefore prevent physical identification.

## Success criterion
A mathematically clean locality-style abstraction useful independently of QFT, or a proof that the analogy collapses without adding a causal relation.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not call the signed number axis spacetime or identify opposite signs with spacelike separation.
