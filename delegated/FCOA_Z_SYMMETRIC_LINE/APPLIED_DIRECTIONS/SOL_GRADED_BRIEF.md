# SOL-GRADED — Scientific Director Brief

## Scope
Lie superalgebras, graded/color algebras, supersymmetry.

## Core question
Can a sign-sector law spectrum generated from one FCOA coordinate axis be represented faithfully as a grading-dependent bracket structure without introducing the grading axiomatically?

## First target
Construct the smallest dictionary between

\[
X^+\sqcup\{0\}\sqcup X^-
\]

and a two-grade algebraic decomposition, then test whether the FCOA sector map can reproduce a graded exchange factor while preserving the fact that the signed sectors originate from reversible completion of one ray.

## Required negative test
Show whether the FCOA origin/reflection construction contains information that cannot be recovered from an abstract \(\mathbb Z_2\)-grading alone.

## Success criterion
A faithful representation theorem or a proof of strict non-equivalence.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not identify FCOA sign with fermion/boson parity merely by analogy.
