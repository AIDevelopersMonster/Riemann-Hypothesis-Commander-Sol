## Final theorem package

Within the fixed-dimensional pure-order provenance class, the published result establishes:

- one simple symmetric irreflexive `C4`-free graph;
- payload carrier of interpretation dimension `2`;
- exact atomic half-graph depth `2`;
- `Theta(N)` primitive incidence cost for the exhibited max-shell order;
- FO-definable full order of type `omega` on the whole payload carrier;
- ordinary addition and multiplication not FO-definable in the recovered order;
- dimension `1` impossible for the linear-cost pure-order package, hence exact minimum dimension `2`;
- exact diagonal-spine hub law `Theta(sqrt(N))` in dimension `2`;
- more generally, the exact fixed-dimensional diagonal-spine law `Theta(N^(1/d))`.

The publication does **not** claim a classification of all finite-signature non-order source geometries, nor universal minimality of linear incidence outside the declared provenance class.
