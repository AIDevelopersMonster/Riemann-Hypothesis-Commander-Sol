# Research Questions

## Umbrella question

**How much arithmetic information survives geometric quantization?**

## Primary questions

1. Which Dirichlet frequencies survive the map from `zeta(1/2+it)` to loop interior to binary lattice occupancy?
2. Is the observed comb invariant under lattice translation and grid resolution?
3. Does it survive analysis using actual zero ordinates without smooth-time warping?
4. Can null models preserving geometry but destroying arithmetic order reproduce the comb?
5. Can shifted-lattice averaging be connected rigorously to Poisson summation?
6. Is there an operational information threshold for spectral recovery?
7. Can Dirichlet phase be decoded from the quantized geometric observable?
8. Does the phenomenon generalize to Dirichlet L-functions?
9. What remains after removing all known Dirichlet and low-order intermodulation frequencies?

## Rule for novelty claims

Never call a spectral line new merely because it appears in a new transform. A novelty claim requires separation from:

- known `log n` Dirichlet frequencies;
- prime-power structure;
- nonlinear mixing such as `log(m/k)`;
- sampling/aliasing artifacts;
- grid and rasterization artifacts;
- smooth-time warping;
- geometry-preserving null models.
