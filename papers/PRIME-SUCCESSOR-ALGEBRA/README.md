## Demonstrated mechanisms

1. **Finite-support coding:** a finite prime-successor segment is encoded by the prime support of one squarefree natural number.
2. **Dilation collapse:** for `R(n)=rad(n!)`, dilation finds a scale at which `x<y` becomes strict divisibility `R(tx) | R(ty)` with `R(tx) != R(ty)`.

The HTML page illustrates the proofs; it is not a substitute for them.

## Scientific status

The manuscript separates classical results, explicit corollaries, observations proved in the note, and open questions. No claim of absolute priority is made for the elementary definability observations without a specialist prior-art audit.
