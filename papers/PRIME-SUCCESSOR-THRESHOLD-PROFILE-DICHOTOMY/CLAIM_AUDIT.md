# Claim Audit — Support-Cardinality Valuation Wall

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** PASS for publication candidate

## Proved

- If `P_+(kappa)={r:kappa(r)>=1}` is finite, `V_{Delta,kappa}` is parameter-free interdefinable with `V_{Delta,0}`.
- In the finite-support phase, ordinary prime order and prime successor remain parameter-free non-definable and every fixed parameter-free ternary isolator has finite GIR.
- If `P_+(kappa)` is infinite, one fixed parameter-free ternary isolator has infinite GIR.
- The infinite-support phase uniformly codes every finite directed graph and its complete first-order theory is undecidable.
- Exact empty-parameter boundary:

  `B_kappa in Def_empty(V_{Delta,0})` iff `|P_+(kappa)|<infinity`.

- Every constant positive depth `m>=1`, every infinite density-zero positive support, and every unbounded positive-depth profile lies on the amplifying side.

## Not claimed

- ordinary prime order or prime successor becomes definable on the amplifying side;
- all infinite-positive-support profiles are mutually interdefinable;
- `B_0` is definable from every infinite-positive-support profile;
- stability, NIP, simplicity, or another global model-theoretic classification;
- a minimal/effective exceptional adelic set;
- historical priority beyond the targeted literature audit.

## Critical proof obligations checked

1. Fixed standard primes are parameter-free definable by threshold crossing under multiplication by a fixed scalar.
2. Open adelic image yields full local factors outside finitely many places.
3. `SL_2(Z_r) -> SL_2(Z/r^k Z)` is surjective for every finite `k`.
4. The integral matrices `A=[[0,-1],[1,1]]` and `I_2` give EDGE/NONEDGE at every positive depth for marker primes `r>3`.
5. Chebotarev is applied only to a finite Galois quotient and trace/determinant are conjugacy-invariant.
6. The Trakhtenbrot translation is syntactic and never computes the external profile `kappa`.
7. Definability statements are explicitly empty-parameter statements.

**Verdict:** PASS.
