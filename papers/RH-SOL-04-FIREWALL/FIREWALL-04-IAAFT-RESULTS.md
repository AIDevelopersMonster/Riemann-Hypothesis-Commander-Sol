## Frozen primary verdict

By the preregistered FIREWALL-04 primary criterion, the test passes on both ranges and both target dictionaries:

- observed exceeds surrogate q99;
- empirical p-value is below `0.01`;
- no generated surrogate reaches observed.

Therefore the exact-target score is not reproduced by the particular frozen 200-iteration IAAFT surrogate family.

## Fidelity limitation

The spectral convergence diagnostic is not small. The median realization-mean relative Fourier-magnitude mismatch is about `17.7%` on loops 1..20000 and `16.8%` on loops 20001..40000. Some individual block mismatches are substantially larger.

This matters scientifically. The result does **not** yet justify the strongest possible statement that an exact amplitude distribution plus a faithfully preserved second-order spectrum is jointly insufficient. Exact distribution preservation is achieved, but second-order spectrum preservation is only approximate at the measured fidelity.

The correct statement at this stage is:

> The frozen 200-iteration IAAFT family, which preserves the exact blockwise area distribution and approximates the original blockwise Fourier magnitude spectrum with about 17% median mean relative mismatch, does not reproduce the observed exact-`log(m)` alignment.

## Why FIREWALL-03 remains important

FIREWALL-03 preserves the Fourier magnitudes exactly but not the amplitude distribution. FIREWALL-04 preserves the amplitude distribution exactly but only approximately the Fourier magnitudes. Taken together they strongly constrain simple marginal-only and spectrum-only explanations, but they do not yet constitute an exact joint-invariant surrogate.

## Next step

A separate preregistered convergence stress test, FIREWALL-04B, will strengthen IAAFT without changing the completed FIREWALL-04 primary run. It will:

- retain the exact area-value multiset;
- use substantially more IAAFT iterations;
- use multiple independent starts;
- retain the rank-projected iterate with the lowest spectral mismatch, selected **only** by the preservation diagnostic and never by the `log(m)` target score;
- impose a preregistered spectral-fidelity gate before any strong joint-preservation interpretation is allowed.

## Guardrails
