## 1. Central result

The branch constructs and analyzes the Prime-Status Quotient

\[
\Xi(n)=(\operatorname{supp}(n),Q(n)),
\]

where `Q(n)` records only whether some prime occurs with exponent at least two.

The quotient preserves prime/composite status exactly while destroying all numerical prime coordinates:

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(\mathfrak P_0)\cong\operatorname{Sym}(\mathbb P).
\]

From this left wall the branch develops a definability corridor through finite periodic phase, nonperiodic symmetry breaking, true successor, labelled weak monadic words, and finally cardinality synchronization.

## 2. Main theorem chain authorized for publication

### A. Exact prime-status quotient

\[
n\text{ prime}\iff |\operatorname{supp}(n)|=1\land Q(n)=0.
\]

The nonunit irreducibles of the quotient are exactly the ordinary primes.

### B. Finite periodic phase wall

Any fixed finite residue-colour expansion leaves infinite within-colour prime permutation groups, so neither standard prime order nor true prime successor is definable.

### C. Nonperiodic intermediate layers

Explicit oriented pair-block relations break prime symmetry strictly while leaving enough block permutations to prevent reconstruction of global prime order.

### D. Finite-injury successor obstruction

A relation can agree with true prime successor on all but at most four edges while still failing to define true successor. Thus density-one agreement is not a reconstruction criterion.

### E. Exact successor/WMSO reduction

With true prime successor and finitely many unary colours, the first-order Prime-Status structure is effectively intertranslatable with weak monadic second-order logic of the corresponding labelled successor word.

### F. Prime-residue recurrence barrier

For the actual mod-4 prime word

\[
w_4(n)=p_n\bmod4,
\]

deciding its monadic theory would decide recurrence of every fixed finite residue pattern. This locates a genuine analytic-number-theory frontier without claiming undecidability.

### G. Finite-Carrier Arithmetic Jump

If coordinate addition of prime indices is added/defined, internal finite supports define coordinate divisibility; Julia Robinson then yields multiplication. Hence the structure interprets full first-order arithmetic and is undecidable.

### H. Maximal-recurrence separation

There exists a computable binary word in which every finite binary pattern occurs infinitely often, yet coordinate addition is not definable. Therefore maximal local recurrence is insufficient for arithmetic recovery.

### I. Equinumerosity synchronization theorem

Adding equality of cardinalities of finite supports yields

\[
|[x,z)|=|[0,y)|
\iff
\operatorname{idx}(z)-\operatorname{idx}(x)=\operatorname{idx}(y),
\]

so coordinate addition becomes definable.

Thus

\[
\operatorname{EqCard}
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Add}_{idx}
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Div}_{idx}
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Mul}_{idx}.
\]

### J. Order-cardinality interaction law

Order alone is decidable; EqCard alone is decidable; together they define arithmetic:

\[
\boxed{
\text{order alone tame; EqCard alone tame; order+EqCard wild}.
}
\]

### K. Pure cardinality zero-one boundary

By transfer of Bès's theorem, every pure support-cardinality relation is either already WMSO-order definable or immediately yields addition and multiplication. There is no intermediate genuinely new pure-cardinality phase.

For unary support-size predicates, ultimate periodicity is the exact boundary.

## 3. Claims explicitly excluded from v1.0

The following are not needed for the publication and are moved to sequel work:

5. any claim that density alone controls successor reconstruction;
