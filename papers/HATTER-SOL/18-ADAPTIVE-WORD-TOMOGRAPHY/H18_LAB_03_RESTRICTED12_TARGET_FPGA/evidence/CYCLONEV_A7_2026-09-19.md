## Same-mathematics architecture comparison on Cyclone V

H18-LAB-03 and H18-LAB-04 are generated from the same restricted-12 H18-11
decision certificate. Their principal difference is execution discipline:

- LAB-03: temporal reuse;
- LAB-04: full spatialization.

Measured Cyclone-V comparison:

| quantity | LAB-03 temporal | LAB-04 spatial | spatial/temporal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ALMs | 1,455 | 10,627 | 7.304 |
| DSP blocks | 26 | 48 | 1.846 |
| registers | 211 | 69 | 0.327 |
| Fmax | 47.02 MHz | 28.52 MHz | 0.607 |
| worst data delay | 21.075 ns | 34.827 ns | 1.653 |
| logic levels | 18 | 30 | 1.667 |
| cell delay | 10.227 ns | 13.556 ns | 1.326 |
| routing delay | 10.850 ns | 21.270 ns | 1.960 |
| worst transaction cycles | 42 | 1 | 0.0238 |

At each design's measured Fmax, the worst transaction times are approximately

\[
T_{\rm seq}
=
\frac{42}{47.02\text{ MHz}}
\approx0.893\,\mu s,
\]

\[
T_{\rm spat}
=
\frac{1}{28.52\text{ MHz}}
\approx0.0351\,\mu s.
\]

Thus temporal LAB-03 is about 7.3 times smaller in ALM usage and uses about
1.85 times fewer DSP blocks, while full spatialization reduces worst
transaction latency by approximately 25.5 times.

This is a clean architecture-effect result for one fixed H18 mathematical
presentation.  It must not be interpreted as a comparison of different
mathematics.

No claim of global area or delay optimality is made.
