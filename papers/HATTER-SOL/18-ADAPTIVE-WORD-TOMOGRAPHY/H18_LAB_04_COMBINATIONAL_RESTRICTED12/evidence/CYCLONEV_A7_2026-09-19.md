- hold slack: +0.375 ns.

The detailed worst setup path is:

\`\`\`text

delay.

100 MHz does not close.  The design is also reported as not fully constrained
for all setup/hold requirements, so the result is used as a matched comparative
benchmark rather than as a board-level timing signoff.

- Fmax 27.85 MHz;
- worst data delay 35.694 ns;
- 34 logic levels;

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ALMs | 7,941 | 10,627 | 1.338 |
| DSP blocks | 40 | 48 | 1.200 |

Thus the current spatialized H18 presentation is about 33.8% larger in ALM
cost and uses 20% more DSP blocks, but its selected critical path is about 2.4%
shorter, its reported logic depth is about 11.8% lower, and its routing delay
is about 7.4% lower.  The H18 cell-delay component is about 6.5% higher.

Neither presentation Pareto-dominates the other on the full physical vector.

This second matched technology point strengthens the H18-12 interpretation:
different finite mathematical presentations of the same abstract tomography
task can map to distinct area/depth/routing tradeoffs even under the same FPGA,
tool flow, registered one-cycle execution discipline, and timing reference.

No claim is made that either mapping is globally area- or delay-optimal.
