\[
K_k=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2^k-1})
\]

produce unbounded arithmetic branching. Under the residue-degree HATTER port lift, unrestricted dense carriers can erase the scalar boundary completely, but carriers constrained to bounded or subcritical orientable genus cannot erase any fixed positive fraction asymptotically.

The central bridge is

\[
\boxed{\text{arithmetic growth} \Longrightarrow \text{topological cost of erasure}.}
\]
