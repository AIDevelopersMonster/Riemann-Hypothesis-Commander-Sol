# HATTER-SOL-13 · UNBOUNDED WORLD DIVERSITY

## Research question

HATTER-SOL-12 established finite world-response coordinates and proved that infinitely many world addresses do not by themselves imply infinitely many independent structural states.

The next question is exact:

> Does there exist a natural arithmetic family of worlds `W_j` and a fixed rational integer `n` for which the canonical HATTER response assumes unboundedly many distinct values?

Define

\[
D_m(n)=\#\{Z_{W_j}(n):1\le j\le m\}.
\]

The primary target is

\[
\boxed{D_m(n)\to\infty.}
\]

A stronger target is unbounded linear response rank

\[
r_m(n)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{span}\{Z_{W_j}(n)-Z_{W_1}(n):2\le j\le m\}\to\infty.
\]

## Mandatory obstruction

If `n` has `s` rational prime divisors and the response depends only on their split/inert vector in quadratic worlds, then at most `2^s` coarse states occur; allowing ramification gives at most `3^s`.

Therefore the H13 theorem cannot come from merely enumerating more quadratic fields while reading only finite split/inert data.

## Candidate world families, in attack order

1. Orders/conductor towers in a fixed quadratic field.
2. Ray-class or residue-structure towers attached canonically to `n`.
3. Ideal-class/principalization behavior of primes above divisors of `n`.
4. Higher-degree number fields where factorization type alone can have growing complexity.
5. Canonical arithmetic groupoids/covering objects if simpler families fail.

## Success criteria

A successful theorem must avoid external labels. Distinctness must arise from intrinsic arithmetic/network data.

Acceptable outcomes:

- fixed-`n` unbounded diversity theorem;
- fixed-`n` no-go theorem for a large natural world class;
- if fixed `n` fails, an explicit `n_k` family with quantified diversity growth.

## First hostile task

Try to kill the easiest candidate: conductor/order variation. Determine whether a fixed rational integer can acquire unboundedly many genuinely different canonical factor/interface responses as the order conductor varies, or whether the local data at primes dividing `n` again saturate after finitely many conductor valuations.

Do not move to coding/applications until this question is settled enough to know whether H12's infinite world jet contains unbounded effective diversity or only redundant coordinates.
