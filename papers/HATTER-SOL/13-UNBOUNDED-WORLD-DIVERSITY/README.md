### Final theorem spine

For the fixed rational prime `2` in the cyclotomic worlds

\[
K_k=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2^k-1}),
\qquad
n_k=\frac{\varphi(2^k-1)}{k},
\]

we have `n_k -> infinity`. Under the declared residue-degree port model:

1. fixed quadratic conductor towers give only finite fixed-`n` element-divisor diversity;
2. the prime-ideal support of the fixed integer `2` is unbounded across the cyclotomic family;
3. connected simple unrestricted carriers have an exact boundary spectrum;
4. bounded/subcritical orientable genus preserves asymptotically all of the port budget;
5. for every fixed `0<eta<=1`, the minimum orientable genus needed to exhaust at least an `eta` fraction of the port budget satisfies

\[
\boxed{\gamma_k(\eta)=\Theta(n_k k).}
\]
