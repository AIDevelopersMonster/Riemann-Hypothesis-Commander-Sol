The publication is a **structural analysis**, not a claimed solution of the underlying automorphism problem for

\[
\Pi=(\mathbb P,D),\qquad D(q,p)\iff q\mid p-1.
\]

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(\Pi)\stackrel{?}{=}\{\mathrm{id}\}
\]

1. Radical-predecessor reduction to the directed prime graph.
2. Multiplicity-tower theorem with exact one-level extension criterion and kernel
   \[
   \prod_S\operatorname{Sym}(X_S).
   \]
3. Inverse-limit description of the global automorphism group.
4. Pinned finite-future indistinguishability / no-go theorem for finite forward motifs.
5. Infinite propagation of every hypothetical nontrivial motion to infinitely many successors and unbounded Pratt height.
6. Density-one movement theorem: every nonidentity automorphism, if one exists, moves a relative-density-one set of primes.
7. Near-identity rank/size rigidity: asymptotically local automorphisms are trivial.
8. Conditional continuum-sized automorphism regime under full exact-support infinitude (FSI).
