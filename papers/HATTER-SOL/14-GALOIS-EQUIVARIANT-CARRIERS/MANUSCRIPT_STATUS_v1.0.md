## Central theorem

For every sufficiently large `k ≡ 15 (mod 30)`, there exist two connected simple capacity-feasible Galois-equivariant carriers in the same cyclotomic world that are isomorphic as abstract graphs and have equal scalar boundary and ordinary topology, but whose first homology modules are non-isomorphic as `Q[Gamma_k]`-modules relative to the fixed arithmetic Galois action.

The separation also holds integrally over `Z`.

## Mandatory qualification

The graph isomorphism is induced by an automorphism of `Gamma_k`; hence the two module structures are twist-related after changing the arithmetic marking. H14 claims loss of the **fixed arithmetic action** when one passes to the unmarked abstract graph. It does not claim inequivalence modulo all automorphisms of `Gamma_k`.

## Supporting results

- equivariant carriers reduce to Cayley graphs;
- complete scalar exhaustion remains possible for all sufficiently large `k`;
- for fixed `eta`, equivariant genus cost is `Theta(n_k k)`;
- the rational cycle character recovers the exact involution fingerprint for a fixed action;
- the `k=8` example is the finite witness;
- the progression `k ≡ 15 (mod 30)` gives the infinite arithmetic witness.

## Final outlook

The paper ends with an explicitly non-theorem Hatter Sol epilogue: moving from abelian quotient/Cayley carriers to non-abelian coset/Schreier carriers, where two labelled ports `a,b` can exhibit path-order defect through the commutator condition

`g(aba^{-1}b^{-1})g^{-1} in D`.

This is presented only as the bridge to the next paper, not as a quantum-entanglement or H14 theorem claim.
