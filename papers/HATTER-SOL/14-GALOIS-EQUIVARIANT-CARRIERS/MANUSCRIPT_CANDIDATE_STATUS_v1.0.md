## Frozen central theorem

For all sufficiently large `k ≡ 15 (mod 30)`, there are two connected simple capacity-feasible Galois-equivariant carriers in the same cyclotomic world that are isomorphic as abstract graphs and therefore share all ordinary graph-isomorphism invariants, but have non-isomorphic first-homology modules relative to the fixed arithmetic `Gamma_k` action. The separation also holds integrally.

## Claim boundary

The abstract graph isomorphism is induced by a group automorphism. The result is therefore a fixed-arithmetic-marking theorem; no inequivalence is claimed after quotienting module data by all `Aut(Gamma_k)` twists.

Cayley reduction and relation-module machinery are classical background. H14 novelty is claimed only for the arithmetic infinite same-graph separation construction and its HATTER structural interpretation.

## Negative controls retained

1. Galois equivariance does not asymptotically forbid complete port-budget exhaustion.
2. Fixed-fraction equivariant genus cost remains `Theta(n_k k)`.
3. The supporting exact-degree argument uses the repaired parity lemma in `PARITY_REPAIR_EXACT_DEGREE.md`.

## Hatter Sol epilogue

The publication candidate ends with an explicitly non-theorem outlook from abelian Cayley carriers to non-abelian Schreier carriers. Two labelled ports `a,b` have route-order defect controlled by a conjugated commutator relative to the decomposition subgroup. This is a bridge to the next project, not a theorem claim of H14.
