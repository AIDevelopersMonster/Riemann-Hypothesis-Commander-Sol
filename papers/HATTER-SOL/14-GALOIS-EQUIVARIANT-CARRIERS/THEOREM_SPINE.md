# HATTER-SOL-14 theorem spine

Working title: **Galois-Equivariant Carriers: Same Graph, Different Arithmetic Homology**

1. Equivariance-to-Cayley classification for the regular arithmetic action. Background theorem.
2. Boundary law `B_f = n_k (k-d)` for degree `d`.
3. Negative control: complete exhaustion remains possible for all sufficiently large `k`.
4. Negative control: for fixed `eta`, equivariant genus cost remains `Theta(n_k k)`.
5. Equivariant cycle-module character fingerprint: relative to a fixed arithmetic action, rational `H_1` records the involution directions and the count of non-involutory inverse-pair directions.
6. Finite witness at `k=8`: same abstract graph and scalar data, different fixed-action equivariant `H_1`.
7. CENTRAL: for all sufficiently large `k = 15 mod 30`, construct abstractly isomorphic Galois-equivariant carriers with identical ordinary graph topology and scalar HATTER data but non-isomorphic rational `Gamma_k`-modules `H_1` for the fixed arithmetic Galois action.
8. Integral corollary: the corresponding `Z[Gamma_k]` cycle lattices are also non-isomorphic.

Mandatory claim boundary: the graph isomorphism is induced by an automorphism of `Gamma_k`; the two modules are related after twisting by that automorphism. The theorem concerns information retained by the fixed arithmetic marking/action and forgotten by the abstract graph.

Final outlook only: non-abelian Galois actions lead from Cayley carriers to Schreier carriers, where two labelled ports can detect path-order commutator defects.
