# HATTER-SOL-14 · Article Architecture v1.0

## Working title

**Galois-Equivariant Carriers: Same Graph, Different Arithmetic Homology**

## Central claim

In an infinite cyclotomic family, the same abstract carrier graph can support different arithmetic regular actions whose first homology modules are non-isomorphic. Thus ordinary graph topology does not determine equivariant arithmetic cycle memory.

10. **Infinite arithmetic same-graph separation** — CENTRAL  
    For all sufficiently large `k = 15 mod 30`, construct abstractly isomorphic Galois-equivariant carriers with non-isomorphic rational equivariant `H_1` modules.

11. **Integral strengthening**

12. **Interpretation and claim discipline**
