# HATTER-SOL-14 · No Large-k Symmetry Obstruction to Complete Exhaustion

**Status:** exact negative theorem layer

Let

\[
m_k=2^k-1,\qquad
\Gamma_k=(\mathbb Z/m_k\mathbb Z)^\times/\langle2\rangle,
\qquad
n_k=|\Gamma_k|=\frac{\varphi(m_k)}k.
\]

By H14.1, connected simple Galois-equivariant carriers are exactly the Cayley graphs `Cay(Gamma_k,S)` with

\[
S=S^{-1},\quad 1\notin S,\quad \langle S\rangle=\Gamma_k.
\]

Complete port-budget exhaustion is equivalent to `|S|=k`.

## Lemma H14.3 — generator-rank bound

Let `omega(m)` denote the number of distinct prime divisors of `m`. Then

\[
d(\Gamma_k)\le \omega(m_k).
\]

### Proof

Since `m_k` is odd, the Chinese remainder theorem gives

\[
(\mathbb Z/m_k\mathbb Z)^\times
\cong
\prod_{p^a\parallel m_k}(\mathbb Z/p^a\mathbb Z)^\times.
\]

For odd prime powers, each unit group is cyclic. Hence the ambient unit group is generated by at most `omega(m_k)` elements. A quotient cannot require more generators. QED.

## Lemma H14.4 — elementary bound on `omega(m_k)`

For every `k>=17`,

\[
2\omega(m_k)<k.
\]

### Proof

Let `r=omega(m_k)`. If `r<=2`, then `2r<=4<k`.

If `r>=3`, the product of `r` distinct odd primes is at least the product of the first `r` odd primes. For `r=3`,

\[
3\cdot5\cdot7=105>4^3.
\]

Every additional odd prime is greater than `4`, so inductively the product of the first `r` odd primes exceeds `4^r`. Thus

\[
2^k-1=m_k>4^r=2^{2r},
\]

which implies `k>2r`. QED.

## Lemma H14.5 — inverse-closed generating seed

For `k>=17`, `Gamma_k` has an inverse-closed generating set `T_k` with

\[
|T_k|<k.
\]

### Proof

Choose at most `r=d(Gamma_k)` generators `a_1,...,a_r`. Put

\[
T_k=\{a_i,a_i^{-1}:1\le i\le r\}\setminus\{1\}.
\]

Then `T_k=T_k^{-1}`, it generates `Gamma_k`, and

\[
|T_k|\le2r\le2\omega(m_k)<k.
\]

QED.

## Theorem H14.6 — exact size `k` exists for all `k>=17`

For every `k>=17`, there exists an inverse-closed generating set

\[
S_k\subset\Gamma_k\setminus\{1\}
\]

with

\[
\boxed{|S_k|=k.}
\]

Consequently there exists a connected simple Galois-equivariant `k`-regular carrier with

\[
\boxed{B_f=0.}
\]

### Proof

Start with the seed `T_k` from H14.5.

If `|T_k|` and `k` have different parity, then `k` must be odd. Since

\[
\varphi(m_k)=n_k k
\]

is even and `k` is odd, `n_k` is even. Therefore `Gamma_k` has a nonidentity involution.

The set of all nonidentity involutions of a finite group has cardinality `2^t-1` inside its elementary 2-torsion subgroup, hence odd. If every involution already lay in `T_k`, the parity contribution of the singleton inversion orbits would make `|T_k|` odd. Thus when `|T_k|` is even, at least one involution lies outside `T_k`; add one such involution. After this optional step we have an inverse-closed generating set `T'_k` with

\[
|T'_k|<k,
\qquad
|T'_k|\equiv k\pmod2.
\]

The complement

\[
\Gamma_k\setminus(\{1\}\cup T'_k)
\]

is invariant under inversion, whose orbits have size `1` (involutions) or `2`.

By the H13 estimate, `n_k>k` for `k>=17`, so there are at least `k-|T'_k|` available nonidentity elements outside `T'_k`. The required number

\[
k-|T'_k|
\]

is even. Any inversion-invariant finite set of size at least an even integer `q` contains a union of inversion orbits of total size exactly `q`: use 2-orbits first and, if necessary, an even number of singleton involution orbits.

Adjoin such orbits to `T'_k`. The resulting `S_k` is inverse-closed, still generates `Gamma_k`, excludes the identity, and has exact cardinality `k`. QED.

## Programme consequence

The initial H14-A conjecture is false in the asymptotic regime:

> Galois symmetry by itself does **not** forbid complete port-budget exhaustion.

The research target therefore moves from existence/nonexistence of saturated equivariant carriers to the **topological cost of equivariant saturation** and, if necessary, equivariant homological memory.
