## Programme line H07 -> H15

H07-H14 progressively separated integer structure, arithmetic world, carrier geometry, observer choice, topology, Galois symmetry, and equivariant homology. H14 stayed in abelian cyclotomic Galois groups and showed that the same abstract graph can retain different homological data relative to a fixed arithmetic action.

H15 removes the abelian hypothesis in the smallest transparent laboratory.

## Dihedral laboratory

Let `p` be an odd prime and

\[
G=D_{2p}=\langle r,s\mid r^p=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle
=C_p\rtimes C_2.
\]

Fix a reflection subgroup

\[
D=\langle s\rangle\cong C_2.
\]

The sector space is the Schreier coset space

\[
X=D\backslash G=\{Dr^i:i\in\mathbb F_p\}.
\]

Two labelled port types act by right multiplication:

\[
R:i\mapsto i+1,
\qquad
S:i\mapsto-i.
\]

Thus the ports do not commute.

## First theorem targets

1. Classify every word in `R,S` on the sector set.
2. Prove the two-port sector-identification theorem using closure words `R^jS`.
3. Prove a lower bound for closure-only identification complexity.
4. Realize the sector model arithmetically in a `D_{2p}` Galois extension using primes with reflection Frobenius.
5. Interpret the unique fixed sector as a genuine prime-factorization branch in the degree-`p` fixed field.
6. Identify the zeta/L-function object naturally attached to the sector permutation representation.

## Port abstraction

A HATTER port is not assumed to be a physical connector. It is a labelled local continuation/decomposition operation.

For ordinary integers, prime factors may be viewed as commuting factor ports. In extension worlds, prime ideals, decomposition branches, Frobenius actions, or Schreier transitions can supply richer ports. Non-abelianity is not claimed for multiplication in `Z`; it arises from the action on factor/decomposition branches in the surrounding arithmetic world.

## Number-theory watch rule

Every new H15 invariant must be checked for a classical number-theoretic shadow: prime splitting, Frobenius class, decomposition group, permutation character, Artin/Hecke L-function, Dedekind zeta, or an analytic counting function.

This includes a long-range watch for possible relevance to zeta-zero questions, including RH/GRH. No RH claim is permitted without a separate theorem. A bridge to a zeta/L-function counts as a structural connection, not progress on RH by itself.
