# HATTER-SOL-15 · Series history 07–14

**Author:** Malachevsky, A.A.  
**ORCID:** https://orcid.org/0009-0008-6009-3196  
**Series:** *Tea Parties in the Additive–Multiplicative World with Hatter Sol* / *Чаепития в аддитивно-мультипликативном мире с Шляпником Sol*  
**Series umbrella DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17996774

This file records the historical path from HATTER-SOL-07 to HATTER-SOL-14 for readers of HATTER-SOL-15. The entries are included for series continuity even when a particular installment is not a logical dependency of the HATTER-SOL-15 proof.

## HATTER-SOL-07 · Free-Port Factorization

Introduces free-port factorization, the parity/granularity law, and the first typed factor-network architecture.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22724185

## HATTER-SOL-08 · Dimensional Factor Architectures

Develops moment-invariant arithmetic containers and world-dependent dimensional factor networks controlled by splitting geometry and localization.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22744644

## HATTER-SOL-09 · World-Dependent Factor Networks and a Prime-Toggle Response Operator

Introduces a world/interface response and a local prime-toggle operation that changes one resolved prime layer without rebuilding the full factor architecture.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22732435

## HATTER-SOL-10 · Ideal Factor Networks Beyond Unique Element Factorization

Extends the network formalism from element factorization to ideal factorization, prime ideals, and class/principalization residues beyond UFD behavior.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22734865

## HATTER-SOL-11 · Orbital Port Filtrations: How Network Geometry Forgets Arithmetic Direction Data

Studies which arithmetic direction labels are lost under orbital/geometric quotienting and isolates a filtration of retained port information.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22746559

## HATTER-SOL-12 · Observer Laws, World Rotation, and Structural Memory of an Integer

Studies what remains structurally recoverable when arithmetic worlds and observer laws change, treating an integer as an object with observer-dependent structural memory.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747698

## HATTER-SOL-13 · Unbounded World Diversity of a Fixed Integer

Shows that a fixed visible arithmetic object can acquire unbounded carrier multiplicity and world complexity across a controlled family of cyclotomic worlds.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22754637

## HATTER-SOL-14 · Galois-Equivariant Carriers: Same Graph, Different Arithmetic Homology

Separates abstract carrier topology from equivariant arithmetic cycle memory: the same graph can support inequivalent regular arithmetic actions with different homology modules.
