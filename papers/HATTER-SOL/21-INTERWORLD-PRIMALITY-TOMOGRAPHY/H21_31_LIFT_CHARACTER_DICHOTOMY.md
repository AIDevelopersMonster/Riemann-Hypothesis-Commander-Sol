## Theorem H21-LC1 — lift-character dichotomy

For every \(z\in D_p\),

\[
\boxed{
\chi_{D_p}(-z)
=
(-1)^{s_p}\chi_{D_p}(z).
}
\]

Hence:

### Odd \(s_p\)

If

\[
s_p\equiv1\pmod2,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
\chi_{D_p}(-z)=-\chi_{D_p}(z).
}
\]

The two norm lifts lie in opposite quadratic-character classes.

Thus the residual lift sheet can be detected by the intrinsic quadratic
character of \(D_p\).

### Even \(s_p\)

If

\[
s_p\equiv0\pmod2,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
\chi_{D_p}(-z)=\chi_{D_p}(z).
}
\]

The two norm lifts lie in the same quadratic-character class.

No homomorphism

\[
D_p\to\{\pm1\}
\]

distinguishes the two members of a norm fiber.

### Proof

Because \((-1)^{s_p}\) is scalar,

\[
(-z)^{s_p}=(-1)^{s_p}z^{s_p}.
\]

The two cases follow immediately. QED.
