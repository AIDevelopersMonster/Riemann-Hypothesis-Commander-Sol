## 1. Arnault 1997 is direct projective/norm-one prior art

François Arnault,
"The Rabin-Monier theorem for Lucas pseudoprimes",
Mathematics of Computation 66 (1997), 869--881.
DOI:

\[
10.1090/S0025-5718-97-00836-3.
\]

Arnault writes the Lucas roots as \(\alpha,\beta\) and introduces

\[
\boxed{
\tau=\alpha\beta^{-1}.
}
\]

He uses the equivalences

\[
n\mid U_k
\iff
\tau^k=1,
\]

and

\[
n\mid V_k
\iff
\tau^k=-1
\]

in the quadratic integer algebra.

The paper explicitly develops the multiplicative group of norm-one elements and
uses the fact that for prime powers its order is

\[
p^{r-1}\left(p-\left(\frac Dp\right)\right).
\]

For \(r=1\), this is precisely the familiar

\[
p-\chi_p
\]

projective/norm-one scale.
