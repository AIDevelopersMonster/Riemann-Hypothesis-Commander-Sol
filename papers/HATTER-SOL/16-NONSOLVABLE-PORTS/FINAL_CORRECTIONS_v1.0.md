## 1. Correct fourth torus moment

The publication formula is

\[
\boxed{
S_4
=28d+4\operatorname{Tr}\rho(K)+4\operatorname{Tr}\rho(K^{-1}),
\qquad K=[A,B].
}
\]

For the real three-dimensional irreducible representation of \(A_5\), \(d=3\) and \(\chi_3(K)=\chi_3(K^{-1})\), so

\[
\boxed{
S_4=84+8\chi_3(K).
}
\]

Any occurrence in an earlier research note of

\[
48+8\chi_3(K)
\]

is superseded and must not be used in the publication manuscript.

The count is: six pure balanced \(A\)-words, six pure balanced \(B\)-words, and twenty-four mixed balanced words; among the mixed words, sixteen contribute the identity, four contribute \(K\), and four contribute \(K^{-1}\). Thus the identity coefficient is \(6+6+16=28\).

## 2. PSL(2,7) scalar-collapse wording

The ordinary real scalar determinant/Mahler observer **collapses** the two order-seven classes \(7A\) and \(7B\). The oriented complex three-dimensional character does not collapse them.

The precise statement is

\[
\mathcal T_{7A}=\mathcal T_{7B}
\]

for the scalar determinant/Mahler type sets, while

\[
\chi_3(7A)=\frac{-1+i\sqrt7}{2},\qquad
\chi_3(7B)=\frac{-1-i\sqrt7}{2}.
\]
