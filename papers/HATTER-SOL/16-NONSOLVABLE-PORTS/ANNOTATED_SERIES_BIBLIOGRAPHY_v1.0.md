## HATTER-SOL-01

**A Tea Party in the Additive–Multiplicative World with Hatter Sol: The Number Line, the Observer, and Two Operations**  
**«Чаепитие в аддитивно-мультипликативном мире с Шляпником Sol: числовая ось, наблюдатель и две операции»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22639237**.

Foundational note of the series. It separates arithmetic structure from the observer used to represent it and sets up the central question: which apparent properties belong to the object, and which to the chosen operations and coordinates?

## HATTER-SOL-02

**Two Teapots, One Cup: “Who Are You?” Among the Primes**  
**«Два чайника, одна чашка: „Кто ты?“ среди простых»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22656414**.

Develops finite probe complexity for prime identification. It contains exact congruence/quadratic-probe orbit counts, the finite-information barrier, sparse rigidifying families, and the exact binary bound \(\kappa_2(S)=\lceil\log_2|S|\rceil\).

## HATTER-SOL-03

**The Cup That Asks Its Own Questions: Anonymous Probes, the Exact Predecessor of a Prime, and Recursive Rigidity**  
**«Чашка, которая задаёт вопросы сама: анонимные пробы, точный предшественник простого и рекурсивная жёсткость»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22679521**.

Moves from externally labelled probes to intrinsic/self-generated probes. It proves exact prime-predecessor rigidity and isolates the radical-predecessor relation as the next rigidity frontier.

## HATTER-SOL-04

**The Cup Forgets Multiplicities: Radical Predecessor, the Multiplicity Tower, and the Cost of a Hypothetical Prime Symmetry**  
**«Чашка забывает кратности: Радикал предшественника, башня кратностей и цена гипотетической симметрии простых»**  
Status: **publication candidate; Zenodo installment DOI pending deposit**.

Studies the directed prime graph \(D(q,p)\iff q\mid p-1\). It builds the multiplicity tower, proves finite-future no-go and infinite-propagation constraints, and shows that any hypothetical nontrivial symmetry must be global and density-one. It does not claim to solve the underlying automorphism problem.

## HATTER-SOL-05

**Who Is Missing from the Table? Empty Fibers, Exact-Support Spectra, and the Survival of the 3↔5 Transposition**  
**«Кого нет за столом? Пустые волокна, спектр точных опор и выживание перестановки 3↔5»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22718278**.

Refines the prime-symmetry problem through exact-support arithmetic. It proves the cardinality wall and finite-fiber compactness and isolates how missing support fibers control survival of local permutations.

## HATTER-SOL-06

**Orbitwise Survival Kernel**  
**«Орбитальное ядро выживания»**  
Status: **repository research layer; no separate installment DOI assigned**.

Identifies the exact support-orbit data relevant to extension of a seed symmetry and localizes the problem to its causal cone. The note separates compact survival mechanisms from noncompact death models.

## HATTER-SOL-07

**Alice in the Land of Free Threads: Factors as Nodes and the Exact Cost of Multiplicative Splitting**  
**«Алиса в Стране Свободных Нитей: множители как узлы и точная цена мультипликативного расщепления»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22724185**.

Introduces the factor-as-node network model. Its exact free-boundary law \(B(G;c)=\sum_i c_i-2|E(G)|\) quantifies the cost of multiplicative splitting and exposes the topology correction through cycle rank.

## HATTER-SOL-08

**From a Line to Space: Dimensional Suppression of Factor-Architecture Sensitivity**  
**«От линии к пространству: размерностное подавление чувствительности факторной архитектуры»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22744644**.

Studies how geometric graph restrictions modify factor-network sensitivity. Paths prohibit refinement inversion, while planar geometry suppresses the response amplitude relative to unrestricted/three-dimensional carriers.

## HATTER-SOL-09

**World-Dependent Factor Networks and a Prime-Toggle Response Operator**  
**«Мирово-зависимые факторные сети и оператор отклика переключения простого»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22732435**.

Composes arithmetic factorization in different quadratic worlds with typed factor networks and Pareto responses. Prime toggles generate a world hypercube on which a graph Laplacian acts as a canonical response operator.

## HATTER-SOL-10

**Ideal Factor Networks Beyond Unique Element Factorization**  
**«Идеальные факторные сети за пределами однозначной факторизации элементов»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22734865**.

Moves the network model from element factorization to prime ideals and principalization witnesses. It introduces multistate nodes, witness switching, exact phase-transition laws, a purity theorem, and residue-enforced mixed phases.

## HATTER-SOL-11

**Orbital Port Filtrations: How Network Geometry Forgets Arithmetic Direction Data**  
**«Орбитальные портовые фильтрации: как геометрия сети забывает данные арифметических направлений»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22746559**.

Separates arithmetic forgetting, network forgetting, and observational forgetting. Exact orbital placements and carrier geometry produce a controlled \(3\to2\to1\) collapse of distinguishable structural states.

## HATTER-SOL-12

**Observer Laws, World Rotation, and Structural Memory of an Integer**  
**«Законы наблюдателя, вращение миров и структурная память целого числа»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22747698**.

Formalizes the joint dependence of visibility on the integer, arithmetic world, carrier, and observer. Planar and toroidal laboratories show that the tomographic dimension itself changes with carrier geometry.

## HATTER-SOL-13

**A Fixed Integer Across Growing Arithmetic Worlds: Unbounded Structural Diversity and the Topological Cost of Erasure**  
**«Фиксированное целое число в растущих арифметических мирах: неограниченное структурное разнообразие и топологическая стоимость стирания»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22754637**.

Keeps the rational integer fixed while the arithmetic world grows. In a cyclotomic family it proves unbounded branching and quantifies the genus cost required to erase a positive fraction of the induced structural response.

## HATTER-SOL-14

**Galois-Equivariant Carriers: Same Graph, Different Arithmetic Homology**  
**«Галуа-эквивариантные носители: один и тот же граф, разная арифметическая гомология»**  
DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22757307**.

Adds Galois equivariance to the carrier. The same abstract graph can support different arithmetic regular actions with non-isomorphic equivariant first-homology modules, showing that graph topology alone does not determine arithmetic cycle memory.

## HATTER-SOL-15

**Non-Abelian Ports: From Dihedral Commutator Holonomy to Universal Mahler Tomography**  
**«Неабелевы порты: от диэдральной коммутаторной голономии к универсальной Mahler-томографии»**  
Status: **final release candidate v0.5; no Zenodo installment DOI assigned as of the H16 assembly**.

Introduces the dihedral two-port laboratory with \([R,S]=R^2\), connecting commutator holonomy, surface relations, non-Abelian Floquet pairing, arithmetic-world reactions, and Mahler observers. Its central certified result is universal first-harmonic dominance for \(\mu\ge4\), yielding full centered primitive Mahler tomography for every odd prime. H15 is the direct mathematical parent of H16.

## Relation of H16 to the series

H16 is the first installment to move the non-Abelian port mechanism from the solvable dihedral laboratory to finite nonsolvable simple groups. It converts the accumulated observer/port language of H01–H15 into a finite engineering specification for \(A_5\) and \(PSL(2,7)\), which H17 is intended to implement in software and hardware.
