## 1. Source-level profiles

### DIRECT12

\[
24
\]

independent permutation-composition nodes, maximum composition depth 3.

### PREFIX19

\[
19
\]

globally shared prefix-composition nodes, maximum composition depth 3.

Hence the source representation has the exact reduction

\[
\boxed{24\to19}.
\]

### NIELSEN12

Ten primitive observers are generated by exact shortest elementary Nielsen
programmes:

\[
18\text{ shear}+3\text{ inverse},
\]

while the two oriented commutators use six direct compositions.

This is a heterogeneous resource vector, not a scalar hardware-cost claim.

## 3. Compiler-forgetting observation

The experiment therefore gives a controlled E0 example in which

\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm source}(DIRECT12)
\neq
S_{\rm source}(PREFIX19)
}
\]

but after the declared unrestricted flatten/opt flow,

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm cells}(DIRECT12)
=
N_{\rm cells}(PREFIX19)
=
4919.
}
\]

Thus source-level common-subexpression sharing does not automatically survive
as a difference in the optimized RTL cell count.

This is not a theorem that the two minimum Boolean circuits are identical.
It is a reproducible statement about this compiler flow.

It directly motivates the H18-12/H19 distinction:

\[
\boxed{
\text{presentation-sensitive realization}
\neq
\text{semantic optimizer image}.
}
\]

## 4. Nielsen presentation

NIELSEN12 remains distinguishable after the same flow:

\[
\boxed{
5230-4919=311
}
\]

additional reported cells relative to DIRECT12/PREFIX19.

The main visible source is the more expensive arithmetic structure induced by
intermediate inversions and shears:

\[
382\ \$mul
\quad\text{versus}\quad
302\ \$mul.
\]

Therefore algebraic semantic compression by Nielsen generators does not imply
a smaller generic RTL image in this realization.

## 5. Claim boundary

This layer proves neither:

- optimality of the Yosys netlist;
- a Boolean circuit lower bound;
- a technology-independent ordering;
- an FPGA area ordering.

It establishes a controlled compiler behavior on three E0-equivalent
presentations.

### Two-stage compiler-forgetting result

The same phenomenon is now observed at two compiler depths:

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
& DIRECT12 & PREFIX19 & NIELSEN12\\
\hline
\text{post-proc/opt cells} & 4919 & 4919 & 5230\\
\text{post-techmap cells} & 63719 & 63719 & 72867
\end{array}
\]

Therefore, for this exact E0 family,

\[
\boxed{
DIRECT12\not\equiv PREFIX19\text{ as source presentations}
}
\]

but

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm opt}(DIRECT12)
\equiv
C_{\rm opt}(PREFIX19)
}
\]

with respect to the measured optimized cell histograms of both tested Yosys
layers.

By contrast,

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm opt}(NIELSEN12)
\neq
C_{\rm opt}(DIRECT12)
}
\]

under the same flows.

This is a compiler-relative empirical equality/inequality, not a theorem of
Boolean-function canonicality.
