# HATTER-SOL-10 residue-response status

Closed structural conclusions:

1. Residue labels are principal integral ideals, avoiding generator/unit ambiguity.
2. Residue-conditioned Pareto fronts are retained before projection.
3. Finite Pareto antichains in N^2 carry the natural idempotent operations: Pareto-union and Pareto-minimal Minkowski sum.
4. The residue-refined response is an element of the corresponding monoid semiring over principal residue ideals.
5. Forgetting residue labels and then Pareto-minimizing exactly recovers the ordinary witness-adaptive response.
6. For block-disjoint multiplication of principal factor systems, residue labels multiply and boundary vectors add, giving an exact convolution law.
7. This convolution law does NOT extend naively to the fully interacting network: cross-edges can strictly improve the response. Full interaction must be treated as a second optimization layer over the block baseline.
8. Weighted support functions give the same separation: block max-plus convolution plus a nonnegative residue-fiber interaction gain.

Publication-safe architecture:

arithmetic witnesses/residues -> exact block convolution -> cross-edge interaction optimization -> connected Pareto response.

Do not claim multiplicativity of the fully interacting response polynomial.
