## Short abstract

HATTER-SOL-17 turns the finite PSL(2,7) tomography specification of HATTER-SOL-16 into a reproducible digital processor. It proves a depth-four one-erasure obstruction, constructs an optimal robust eight-probe family inside the investigated depth-five candidate space, builds a structural class engine, a 14-composition shared word DAG and ROM-free known-erasure repair, and measures the hardware effect of group closure. Under one technology-independent Yosys methodology, the full flat orbit-ID core decreases from 43,330 to 24,730 Boolean cells and the ROM-free fingerprint core from 42,484 to 23,937. Target FPGA measurements are explicitly not claimed. The paper closes with research programs toward independent probe fabrics, adaptive word tomography and Nielsen dynamics, PUF/Hardware Root-of-Trust, equivariant graph learning, quantum syndrome symmetry compression, discrete Lie-group automata, streaming signatures and attack-first non-Abelian cryptography.
