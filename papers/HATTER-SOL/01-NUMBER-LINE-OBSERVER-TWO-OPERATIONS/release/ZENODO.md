## Description

A reflective mathematical essay about the distinction between arithmetic representation and arithmetic structure. The paper starts from the apparent irregularity of prime numbers on the ordinary number line and separates the roles of distinguishability, iteration, natural-number structure, addition, multiplication, divisibility, and primality. Its strict algebraic hinge is the contrast between the full prime-permutation automorphism group of the multiplicative monoid of positive integers and the rigidity of the full natural arithmetic structure. The final section introduces the paired map Omega(a,b)=(a+b,ab) only as a doorway to a future research programme on reducts, structural information loss, and the recovery of prime symmetry.
