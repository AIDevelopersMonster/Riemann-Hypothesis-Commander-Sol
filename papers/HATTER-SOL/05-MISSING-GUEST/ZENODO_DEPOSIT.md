## Description / abstract

We study the directed graph on the primes

\[
\Pi=(\mathbb P,D),\qquad D(q,p)\iff q\mid p-1,
\]

which remembers the exact set of prime predecessors while forgetting multiplicities. The paper studies the first nontrivial local transposition \(3\leftrightarrow5\) and proves a structural package: finite fixed-divisor escape for exact-support candidate families; a cyclotomic dimension jump; a rigorous 3-versus-5 local sieve asymmetry with weighted asymptotics; hereditary descendant sieve pressure and the cardinality wall; finite-fiber compactness and finite obstruction trees under FFC; density-one forward cones, already at directed distance at most two; and a Causal Survival Theorem under Cone-Fiber Infinitude (CFI). The central question

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(\Pi)\stackrel?=\{\mathrm{id}\}
\]

remains open.
