# HATTER-SOL-02 · First Strike

## One congruence can name an arbitrary finite set of primes

Let \(m>1\), and let

\[
E_m(x,y)\iff x\equiv y\pmod m.
\]

Consider the expansion of the multiplicative monoid

\[
\mathcal M_m=(\mathbb N_{>0},\times,E_m).
\]

Every automorphism of \((\mathbb N_{>0},\times)\) is induced by a permutation of the primes. The question is: which primes become individually fixed when the single congruence relation \(E_m\) is added?

## Theorem 1 · Exact fixed-prime theorem

For every integer \(m>1\),

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Fix}_{\mathbb P}(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m))
=
\{p\in\mathbb P:p\mid m\}.
}
\]

In words: **the primes fixed by every automorphism of \((\mathbb N_{>0},\times,\equiv_m)\) are exactly the prime divisors of \(m\).**

### Proof

Let \(\Phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m)\). Since \(\Phi\) preserves multiplication, it permutes the prime atoms.

Because \(\Phi\) also preserves \(E_m\), it induces an automorphism

\[
\bar\Phi:(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z,\times)\to(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z,\times)
\]

of the finite multiplicative monoid of residue classes.

Let \(p\mid m\) be prime and write \(\Phi(p)=q\), with \(q\) prime. The residue class \([p]_m\) is a nonunit. Monoid automorphisms preserve the group of units, hence \([q]_m\) is also a nonunit. Since \(q\) is prime, this implies \(q\mid m\). Thus \(\Phi\) permutes the prime divisors of \(m\).

Now consider the principal ideal generated by \([p]_m\):

\[
[p]_m(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z).
\]

Its cardinality is

\[
\frac{m}{\gcd(m,p)}=\frac mp,
\]

because \(p\mid m\). Automorphisms preserve principal ideals and their cardinalities. Hence, if \(\Phi(p)=q\), then

\[
\frac mp=\frac mq,
\]

so \(p=q\). Therefore every prime divisor of \(m\) is fixed individually.

Conversely, let \(q\nmid m\) be prime. Since \(\gcd(q,m)=1\), Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions gives infinitely many primes

\[
r\equiv q\pmod m.
\]

Choose \(r\ne q\). Swap the prime atoms \(q\) and \(r\), fixing every other prime, and extend this permutation multiplicatively to \(\mathbb N_{>0}\). Because \(q\equiv r\pmod m\), this automorphism preserves every residue class modulo \(m\), and therefore preserves \(E_m\). It moves \(q\).

Thus no prime outside the support of \(m\) is fixed by all automorphisms. Hence

\[
\operatorname{Fix}_{\mathbb P}(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m))
=
\{p:p\mid m\}.
\]

\(\square\)

## Corollary 2 · Arbitrary finite prime individuality

Let \(F\subset\mathbb P\) be finite and nonempty. Put

\[
m_F=\prod_{p\in F}p.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Fix}_{\mathbb P}(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_{m_F}))=F.
}
\]

So **one named congruence relation is sufficient to individuate any prescribed finite set of primes, and no others.**

## Corollary 3 · Residual symmetry remains infinite

For every unit residue class \(a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\), define

\[
C_a=\{q\in\mathbb P:q\equiv a\pmod m\}.
\]

Dirichlet's theorem implies that every \(C_a\) is infinite. Any permutation of primes inside a fixed \(C_a\), extended multiplicatively and fixing all other primes, lies in \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m)\).

Therefore

\[
\prod_{a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times}
\operatorname{Sym}(C_a)
\le
\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m).
\]

Thus a finite congruence layer can create finitely many individually named primes while leaving an enormous infinite symmetry among the rest.

## Exact support cost inside the congruence family

The theorem immediately exposes a subtlety: **counting relations is a bad measure of information.** One relation \(E_m\) can encode arbitrarily many primes by choosing \(m\) with many distinct prime factors.

For a finite nonempty \(F\subset\mathbb P\), define the modulus cost

\[
\kappa(F)
=
\min\{\log m:
\operatorname{Fix}_{\mathbb P}(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m))=F\}.
\]

By Theorem 1, the admissible moduli are exactly those with prime support \(F\), so the smallest is the squarefree kernel

\[
\operatorname{rad}(m)=\prod_{p\in F}p.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\kappa(F)=\sum_{p\in F}\log p.
}
\]

This quantity is not proposed as a universal arithmetic information invariant; it is the **exact cost inside this specific congruence-reduct family**.

## Conceptual interpretation for the series

HATTER-SOL-01 compared two extremes:

\[
\operatorname{Sym}(\mathbb P)
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\{\mathrm{id}\}.
\]

The present theorem supplies a genuine intermediate layer. A single periodic fragment of additive information does not restore addition, but it can individuate a precisely prescribed finite set of multiplicative atoms.

The tea-party translation is now mathematically controlled:

- the multiplication teapot alone leaves all primes interchangeable;
- the congruence \(E_m\) is a finite-resolution pour from the additive teapot;
- in the cup, exactly the prime ingredients dividing \(m\) become individually recognizable;
- all remaining prime ingredients still occur in infinite indistinguishability classes.

The important surprise is that **one pour is not one bit**: the complexity of the pour is carried by the modulus \(m\).

## Next strike

The next natural question is stronger than the original finite-family problem:

\[
\boxed{
\text{What is the full prime-orbit decomposition of }\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal M_m)?
}
\]

For primes coprime to \(m\), residue classes give obvious infinite suborbits, but automorphisms of the finite multiplicative monoid \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z,\times)\) may merge residue classes. The next task is to determine exactly which unit residue classes can be coupled by a lift to a permutation of prime atoms.

A second independent direction is to replace \(E_m\) by weaker periodic predicates and determine the minimal definable additive leakage needed to fix a prescribed finite prime set.

## Literature status

A preliminary search found standard literature on automorphism groups of cyclic groups, multiplicative residue structures, Presburger arithmetic, and general reduct/expansion theory, but no exact match yet for the fixed-prime theorem above in the structure \((\mathbb N_{>0},\times,\equiv_m)\). This is **not yet a novelty claim**. A dedicated literature audit remains mandatory before publication.
