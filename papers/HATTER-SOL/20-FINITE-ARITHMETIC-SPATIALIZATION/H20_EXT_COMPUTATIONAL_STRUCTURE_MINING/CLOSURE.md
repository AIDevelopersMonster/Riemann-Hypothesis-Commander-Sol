## Final decision

The H20-EXT computational structure-mining programme is closed.

The explored observer families produced reproducible finite effects, but no structural object was found that is strong, stable and interpretation-independent enough to justify a new theorem line or publication.

In particular:

- continuation-quotient compression was explained by wheel/residue structure;
- the low-degree Walsh deficit survived several strong controls but failed to remain invariant under stronger conditioning;
- the conditioning-resolution crossover failed fresh cross-width validation;
- no robust prime-specific invariant emerged.

Therefore no further observer mining is justified inside H20-EXT.

## What remains

The branch is retained only as an archive of:

- exact finite computations;
- falsification protocols;
- reproducible CPU tools;
- workflow records;
- negative results;
- exact conditional-expectation formulas.

These are supporting research records, not the basis of a paper.

## Reopening rule

Do not reopen H20-EXT by adding another nearby Boolean metric, another wheel size, another block threshold, or another post-hoc fit.

Reopen only if an independent external theorem, new representation, or genuinely new computational witness changes the research question materially.
