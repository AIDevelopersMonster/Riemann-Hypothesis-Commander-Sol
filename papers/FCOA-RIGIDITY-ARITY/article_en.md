## Main result

For complete off-diagonal terminal layers with anonymous output fibers, in the natural class of reducts determined by anonymous equality patterns on bounded-size labeled carrier subsets,

\[
\boxed{q=2:\ k_{\mathrm{exact}}=3,\qquad q\ge3:\ k_{\mathrm{exact}}=4.}
\]
