# Claims and limitations

## What RH-SOL-01 reports

1. A concrete binary geometric observable is defined from consecutive critical-line Argand loops and the Cartesian integer lattice.
2. In the archived 10,000-loop dataset, the double-centered binary tensor exhibits a strong physical-frequency comb near `omega = log(m)`.
3. After removal of principal log-integer components, leading residual structure is compatible with nonlinear difference frequencies `log(m/k)`.
4. A predeclared log-integer plus small-ratio dictionary reduces out-of-sample variance by about 36.3% under even/odd cross-fit.
5. Simple static parity/mod-3/mod-5 hypotheses and a naive von-Mangoldt spatial effect do not survive the reported controls.
6. The smooth zero-density model yields the sampling boundary `T = 2*pi*m^2` and reciprocal first-fold label `m* = T/(2*pi*m)`.

## What RH-SOL-01 does not claim

- It does not prove the Riemann Hypothesis.
- It does not derive the functional equation.
- It does not explain the distribution of zeta zeros.
- It does not claim that Nyquist aliasing causes the Riemann-Siegel formula.
- It does not claim that `T = 2*pi*m^2` is a universal power-onset threshold.
- It does not establish that every observed residual line is arithmetic rather than partly generated by nonlinear quantization.
- It does not establish novelty for sampling theory applied to zeta in general.

## Known methodological limitations of the published version

The published note is intentionally exploratory. Important follow-up questions include:

- formal treatment of self-intersecting loop interiors and boundary points;
- full upstream regeneration from zero ordinates to the binary tensor;
- tests using actual `gamma_n` rather than only smooth-time warping;
- shifted-lattice sensitivity;
- geometry-preserving, order-randomized and phase-randomized null models;
- independent-height confirmation;
- random-dictionary controls for the frequency-removal step.

These are not silently patched into the published article. They define the next RH-SOL branches.

## Interpretation discipline

The durable claim is about **information survival under severe geometric quantization**. Any stronger statement about zeta, the Riemann-Siegel formula or RH requires additional evidence or proof.
