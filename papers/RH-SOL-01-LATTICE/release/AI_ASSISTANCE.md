# AI assistance disclosure

This work was developed as a human-AI exploratory mathematics workflow.

- The human researcher selected the Riemann-zeta Argand-loop object, the Cartesian integer lattice, and the scientific direction, and made the publication decision.
- **Commander Sol (OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol)** assisted with hypothesis generation, code generation, computational tests, negative controls, spectral interpretation, literature search, reproducibility packaging, and manuscript drafting.
- An independent **Anthropic Claude** cross-model check supplied an important correction: the boundary `T = 2*pi*m^2` should be interpreted as an aliasing / identifiability boundary, not as a necessary power-onset law.
- The reciprocal first-fold map `m* = T/(2*pi*m)` and its fixed-point comparison with the symmetric Riemann-Siegel scale were developed in subsequent Commander Sol analysis.

AI systems are not authors. Mathematical and computational claims should be judged from the formulas, archived data, scripts, controls, and reproducibility materials.
