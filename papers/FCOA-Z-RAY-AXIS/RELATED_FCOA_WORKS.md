# FCOA programme context and related works

## Current work

**Alex Malachevsky.** *Reflections on How a Ray Becomes an Axis: And why old operations reveal new local laws after a second direction appears*  
Zenodo DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22171473**

This FCOA-Z paper should be read after the following two published FCOA records.

1. **Alex Malachevsky.** *Reflections on Admissibility Geometry with Commander Sol: How a Partial Operation Remembers an Oriented Carrier.* Zenodo, 2026. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22129787.
2. **Alex Malachevsky.** *Reflections on Value-Rigidity with Commander Sol: Two Anonymous Outputs, Identity Digraphs, and Sparse Rigid Fibers.* Zenodo, 2026. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22160014.

Programme progression:

`domain geometry / structural memory -> value-rigidity / sparse rigid fibers -> reversible carrier / local-law differentiation`

The present paper does not replace the earlier definitions of FCOA. It studies a continuation problem for a legacy FCOA operation after the carrier acquires a second direction.
