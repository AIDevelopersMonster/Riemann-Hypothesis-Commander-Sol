# Delegated FCOA Research Directions — Scientific Director Registry

**Scientific director:** main Commander Sol line  
**Purpose:** parallelize worthwhile side directions without allowing subordinate branches to steer the central programme by momentum alone.

## Governance principle

Delegated chats are **scientific supervisors of their own directions**, not co-equal directors of the FCOA programme.

A subordinate result enters the main line only after explicit review by the main scientific director.

The director will not adopt a result merely because a delegated branch produced a long development, a persuasive narrative, or a locally coherent programme.

## Upstream admission filter

A subordinate result is considered for the main line only if it satisfies most of the following:

1. mathematically correct with proof-level scope;
2. independently checked or hostile-audited when the claim is nontrivial;
3. materially sharper than what the main line already knows;
4. not a restatement of existing FCOA language;
5. does not silently import ordinary arithmetic or external labels;
6. comes with exact signature/sort assumptions;
7. survives the relevant Carrier-/Domain-/Value-Erasure distinction;
8. has explicit small-case checks and counterexample search where finite;
9. contributes to the strategic question of structural memory or its limits;
10. can be stated cleanly enough to justify the added complexity.

Interesting but peripheral results remain in their delegated branch.

## Central line retained by the director

The main line retains control of:

\[
\boxed{
G4\ \text{audit}
\longrightarrow
\text{bounded-output amplification}
\longrightarrow
\text{Arithmetic Leakage boundary}
}
\]

Arithmetic Leakage is not delegated as the primary programme because it is expected to become a central phase-boundary question for FCOA.

## Reporting protocol

Every delegated supervisor should maintain an `UPSTREAM_MEMO.md` containing only results that they believe deserve director review.

The main director will periodically inspect these memos. Absence of upstream adoption does not imply a local result is wrong; it may simply be strategically peripheral.

No delegated branch may modify another delegated branch or the main FCOA files unless explicitly instructed.

## Interpretation of P2 recommendations

The director adopts from P2 the following methodological principles:

- FCOA is treated as a laboratory of structural memory, not as a replacement arithmetic;
- UNDEF is structural absence, not a value;
- domain, values, result sorts, anchors, and erasure are separate information channels;
- every branch should have a passport and a diff from its predecessor;
- atomicity must be sandbox-relative;
- new cells must justify exactly what symmetry/information they change.

The director does **not** adopt P2's suggestion that G3-A should automatically replace the central programme. G3-A is an important mechanism, but the main line has already moved to the stronger G4 bounded-output amplification question and then to the Arithmetic Leakage boundary.
