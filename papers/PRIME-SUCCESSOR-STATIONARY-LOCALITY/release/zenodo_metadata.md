## Description

This paper develops a finite multi-place extension of the fixed-ball interior construction in the Prime-Successor Algebra / Two Walls programme. We study two-sorted structures

\[
\mathcal B_{u,S}=\Bigl((\mathbb N_{>0},\times),(\mathbb Q,+,0,(B_\ell)_{\ell\in S}),U\Bigr),
\]

where \(S\) is a finite set of rational primes, \(B_\ell(x)\iff v_\ell(x)\ge0\), and prime atoms are linked to rational labels satisfying Private-Place separation.

The target additive structure admits a finite-depth multi-place normal form. Exact relations among regular prime labels are controlled by injective private denominator places, while non-pinned target witnesses can be moved inside finite-depth cells by fresh private places lying outside the stationary atlas. This yields the Finite Stationary Locality Theorem and Formula-Relative Tail Symmetry.

Consequences include non-definability of the standard prime order and prime-successor relation and finiteness of Grid-Isolation Rank for every fixed isolator formula. For the Ramanujan labels

\[
u_p=(\tau(p)^2-p^{11})/p^{11},
\]

the hypotheses hold for every finite stationary atlas. The paper also shows that an infinite family of separately named stationary predicates remains formula-by-formula finite; the next open boundary is a uniformly indexed locality relation \(\mathsf B(\ell,x)\), where the place becomes a first-order variable.
